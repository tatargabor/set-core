Wave Board

Waiting on people

This board shows the live status of a job that runs in waves. Nothing has been pushed to it yet: the agent pushes the state after set-wave-board sync, and it appears here without a reload.

Status comes from evidence in the repository (task boxes, files, commits); an agent's own report fills the gaps until evidence speaks. A decision is a choice a person has to make; a blocker is work or access someone else has to provide. Updated by set-wave-board. On this page you can set an item's status and assignee, and answer what it waits on; the agent takes them over into the repository.
